Gridgets

Grid-based desktop widgets for GNOME Shell.
Snap widgets to a clean grid and bring your desktop to life.

GitHub GNOME Extensions

Features

Everything you need to make your desktop yours.

Grid Alignment

Snap widgets cleanly to a responsive desktop grid. No more floating windows cluttering your workspace.

Individual Styling

Customize colors, fonts, border radii, and border widths for every single widget to match your setup.

Themes

Switch the look of all your widgets in one click. Pick from curated themes or build your own palette.

Multi Monitor Settings

Place widgets across every display. The grid adapts per monitor so your layout stays consistent on any screen.
